## The problem

When I joined CoLearn in May 2021, Tanya, its free homework help, ran on WhatsApp with ~1.01M users. A student photographed a textbook question and got a video solution back. But it lived outside the app, where CoLearn sold its live classes.

## How I approached it

I built Tanya in the app from 0 to 1, working across several teams.

- **Moved students into the app.** I capped WhatsApp at 3 questions per 24 hours, after which students needed the app.
- **Took answer match accuracy from 40% to 85%.** We expanded the question bank to Physics, Chemistry and grades 6–10, and added image-quality checks for blurry and handwritten photos.
- **Removed dead ends.** An unmatched question led to a similar question, the concept section for that topic, or a trial class on it, which doubled as a path into paid.
- **Built for real students.** The Coco and Neco characters made solution videos fun, and a small app size kept it working on low-end Android phones.

## The hard call

Tanya grew fast but didn't convert: only ~2% of users booked a trial. I built the case for the C-levels to sunset Tanya in the app and move its videos to YouTube. That kept free discovery without the running cost, and let us focus on referrals and live classes.

## Outcome

- **15K users in launch week (August) to ~1.2M MAU by December**, and ~5M total users.
- **Answer match accuracy from 40% to 85%.**
- ~9.7M video views a month, up to 27.8M in exam months.

## What I learned

I optimised Tanya for MAU, the metric I could move, instead of paid conversion, the one that mattered. Now I set the revenue metric as the north star from day one.
