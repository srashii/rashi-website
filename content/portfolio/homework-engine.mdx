## The problem

CoLearn sells live classes to students in grades 4 to 12. Paid acquisition was expensive, and students who did sign up often missed classes and churned before the semester ended.

## How I approached it

- **Led with a free, useful tool.** I built a photo-to-solution homework engine from scratch: students snap a question and ML image matching finds the worked solution in a proprietary question bank.
- **Turned free users into learners.** Iterative A/B tests on the trial-to-paid funnel amplified trial subscriptions 5X in three months.
- **Made showing up rewarding.** Instant-gratification rewards and in-class engagement experiments raised class attendance from 58% to over 90% for most students.
- **Removed friction behind the scenes.** Automated onboarding, an in-app chatbot and a classroom tracking system that powered a teacher performance index.

## Outcome

- The homework engine reached millions of students every month and became CoLearn's top-of-funnel.
- Semester renewal reached 90% for paid subscribers.
- Onboarding time fell by 97% and weekly support time by 60%.
