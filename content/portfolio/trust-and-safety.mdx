## The problem

On a super-app where people both talk and pay, trust is the product. Botim had no Trust & Safety framework across chat and calls, so spam callers and abusive accounts were handled case by case through customer support.

## How I approached it

- **Created the framework first.** I wrote the cross-squad Trust & Safety workbook (initiatives, metrics and open decisions) that the workstream now runs on.
- **Gave users a voice.** I designed the report-a-message flow end to end, from the action sheet to reason chips to a post-report option to block, and locked the product decisions before handing over to engineering.
- **Made enforcement proportional.** I scoped a volume-based, tiered enforcement system for one-to-one and group chats, so repeat offenders face escalating consequences.
- **Signalled who to trust.** I shipped verified identity and trust indicators so users can tell a real business or contact from an impersonator.

## Outcome

- **Spam-caller complaints to support dropped 65%, from 3.7 to 1.3 per day.**
- Trust & Safety went from ad-hoc tickets to an owned, measurable product area with enforcement, reporting and verified identity in place.
