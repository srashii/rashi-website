## The problem

CoLearn sold small after-school live classes with teachers and mentors. When I joined, ~10K students paid. Students used the product, but parents paid for it, and they doubted online classes worked as well as a tutoring centre.

## How I approached it

As Senior PM I owned converting free users to paid, and as Lead PM I owned retention of paid students, leading 3 PMs, 2 designers and a UX researcher.

**Built conversion around parents and referrals.**

- After each class, students could send a summary to their parents, the people who paid.
- Bring a Friend in class, and referral rewards: a 1:1 teacher session for the referrer and vouchers for lower grades. Referral became our biggest channel.
- Testimonials and NPS shown to parents, and a lower minimum price of about $7 a semester.

**Built retention around the most common parent complaint: not knowing how their child was doing.**

- Weekly parent reports on progress and participation, and alerts when a student missed a class.
- Early renewal tied to teacher choice. The sooner students renewed, the sooner they could pick last semester's teacher, so continuity became a reason to renew early.
- The next class and its Join button on the home screen, so new students made it to their first class without help.
- Automated payment reminders, and dashboards so ops knew which parents to call.

## Outcome

- **Paid students grew from ~10K to ~200K per semester in about two years.**
- **About $2M a year in live-class revenue.**
- **Semester renewal reached 75% organically.**
- Class attendance rose from 58% to over 90% for 75% of students.
