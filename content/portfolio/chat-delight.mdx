## The problem

Chat engagement on Botim lagged behind the apps people use every day. Messaging felt functional rather than personal, and small frictions (a cramped input bar, basic voice notes, no way to celebrate a moment) gave users little reason to keep conversations on Botim.

## How I approached it

- **Made chats feel like the moment.** I introduced chat themes, set per chat from the contact profile with a live preview, and festive themes like Halloween offered through an in-chat prompt, pinned to the top of the theme list during the festival and moved down once it ends. Celebration animations mark birthdays and congratulations.
- **Gave people new ways to express themselves.** I brought GIFs into chat through a rich-media integration, alongside stickers and other expressive formats.
- **Invested in the most-used media.** Voice notes are the most used media type on Botim chat, so I prioritised voice-note enhancements that make recording and listening smoother.
- **Sweated the small details.** I increased the font size on the input bar so typing is easier, and shipped a steady stream of chat UI improvements, from Reply-to on all media types to a smoother notification opt-in.
- **Kept it reliable as features grew.** Delivery quality improvements and a version-compatibility framework made sure new features degrade gracefully on older app versions.

## Outcome

- **Messages sent per user per day grew from 18.5 to 23.8 in six months (~29%).**
- Chat became a stickier daily habit, and a better place to introduce money moments like gifting and split bill.
