## The problem

I joined Botim as Product Manager for Growth & Cross-Sell. Botim was known as a calling app, and only 3.5% of monthly active users made a fintech transaction. More campaigns were not moving that number, so before building anything I looked for why. I found three blockers:

- **Users didn't know Botim had fintech at all.** User research showed this was the biggest problem. People saw Botim as a way to call home, not a place to send money, borrow or buy gold.
- **Growth had no flexible levers.** The promotion engine worked for one service only, there were no dynamic segments, and every campaign meant hours of setup and a long approval chain.
- **Nobody could see user journeys.** We knew how many people transacted, but not where they dropped off.

## How I approached it

**1. Fixed discovery first, by putting money moments where users already were.**

- A dedicated Money tab that explains each service, with a live currency calculator for international transfers.
- For You and Discover sections on the homepage, showing the services most relevant to each user.
- Splash screens for campaigns and moments like Akshaya Tritiya gold offers.
- A "Send money to [country]" button inside the chat composer and an interactive wallet widget on the post-call screen.

Fixing awareness before spending on rewards was the key call: users entering fintech services more than doubled in a month, and grew almost 4X within 6–8 months.

**2. Gave Growth levers they could pull without engineering.**

- **Boost**, a reusable rewards engine (do X, get Y) for cashback, gold and VIP subscriptions, later extended into lending. See the Boost case study.
- **MoEngage** for segmentation and personalised in-app campaigns. Scaling it wasn't my job, but my goals depended on it, so I worked with DevOps to take campaign infrastructure from 200 to 600 QPS and added a queuing mechanism.
- **An in-house Segment Builder** after outages with the third-party tool, cutting campaign setup from 4–5 hours to under 3 minutes.

**3. Made data visible across the company.**

It wasn't my KPI, but I led the Amplitude rollout across 12 squads: I got the CTO's sign-off during a re-architecture, ran company-wide training and office hours, and pushed every team to track a "bad funnel" of errors, not just conversions.

## Outcome

- **Fintech transacting users grew from 3.5% to 8% of monthly active users in one year.**
- **Users entering fintech services more than doubled in a month**, and grew almost 4X within 6–8 months.
- **Campaign infrastructure scaled 3X** (200 to 600 QPS), and **campaign setup went from 4–5 hours to under 3 minutes.**
- **All 12 squads** now make decisions on one analytics platform.
- My scope grew from Growth & Cross-Sell to lending at six months, and to the Chats and Homepage squads at one year.
