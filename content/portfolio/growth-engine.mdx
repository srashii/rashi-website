## The problem

Botim's fintech services were hard to discover, and the Growth team could not move fast: each campaign took four to five hours of manual setup, and many key user actions were not tracked, so nobody could say which ideas actually worked.

## How I approached it

- **Measured everything that matters.** I authored the event-tracking specs across 10+ fintech surfaces (checkout, add funds, P2P, withdrawals, onboarding and more) on Amplitude and MoEngage.
- **Built the tools Growth needed.** I owned an in-house Segment Builder and in-app campaign engine with screen-based triggers, so campaigns fire for the right users on the right screen.
- **Created discovery surfaces.** Splash screens, homepage recommendations, call-end cross-sell widgets and Smart Tips in call and chat turned high-attention moments into fintech entry points.
- **Rewarded the first transaction.** I launched Gold and Silver as redeemable rewards and integrated Boost rewards into lending.

## Outcome

- The share of monthly active users who transact on fintech more than doubled in one year.
- Campaign setup went from hours to under 3 minutes.
- EasyCash lending utilisation rose 68%, and 38% of rewarded customers made a first Gold or Silver purchase within a month.
