## The problem

Botim's fintech services were hard to discover, and the Growth team could not move fast: each campaign took four to five hours of manual setup, and many key user actions were not tracked, so nobody could say which ideas actually worked.

## How I approached it

- **Measured everything that matters.** I authored the event-tracking specs across 10+ fintech surfaces (checkout, add funds, P2P, withdrawals, onboarding and more) on Amplitude and MoEngage.
- **Built the tools Growth needed.** I owned an in-house Segment Builder and in-app campaign engine with screen-based triggers, so campaigns fire for the right users on the right screen.
- **Created discovery surfaces.** Splash screens, homepage recommendations and Smart Tips in call and chat turned high-attention moments into fintech entry points.
- **Made the end of a call an entry point.** Interactive call-end widgets invite users to pull down and explore a fintech service, like a live exchange rate with a one-tap transfer, at the moment they are most engaged.
- **Rewarded the first transaction.** I built the Boost rewards system that turns a user's first transfer, gold purchase or loan into a goal worth completing (see the Boost case study).

## Outcome

- **Fintech transacting users grew from 3.5% to 8% of monthly active users in one year.**
- **Campaign setup went from 4–5 hours to under 3 minutes.**
- Analytics now cover 10+ fintech surfaces end to end.
