## The problem

A super-app homepage has to serve calling users, wallet users, borrowers and investors at once. Botim's homepage showed the same static tiles to everyone, so it did little to help users act on their finances.

## How I approached it

- **Phase 1: actionable widgets.** I defined a widget suite for international transfers, wallet, credit, quick access and an action-needed widget for overdue payments, each tied to a clear next step.
- **Phase 2: real-time by design.** I am leading an event-bus architecture that pushes each user's live financial data (wallet and card, rates, wealth, remittances) straight to homepage widgets.
- **Contextual, not generic.** Credit widgets change by user state, invest widgets show live rates, and the For You feed is configurable by Growth without engineering cycles.
