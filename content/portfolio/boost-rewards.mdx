## The problem

Botim's fintech services had plenty of users who were one step away from their first transaction. Blanket cashback was expensive and attracted bargain hunters, and nothing connected a reward to the behaviour we actually wanted: a first international transfer, a first gold purchase or a first loan.

## How I approached it

- **Turned rewards into goals.** Boost cards set a clear task, such as "Send AED 2500 abroad to get AED 10 cashback", with live progress on the card, so users always know how close they are.
- **Designed one system for many tasks.** I defined three reward mechanics (milestones, accumulators and counters) with consistent default, in-progress and completed states, so any product team can launch a new Boost without new design work.
- **Put rewards where decisions happen.** Boost cards sit on the Botim Pay home, inside the transfer flow and in rewards, and the reward lands in the wallet with a clear "Reward unlocked" moment.
- **Rewarded the behaviour, not the click.** Rewards plug into lending (EasyCash), precious metals and VIP subscriptions, including Gold and Silver as redeemable rewards, so each Boost moves a real product metric. Every reward has two honest end states, "Reward unlocked!" or "Reward on the way!", and the cashback shows in transaction history.

## Outcome

- **EasyCash lending utilisation rose 68%** in a quarter after Boost rewards were integrated into lending.
- **38% of rewarded customers made a first Gold or Silver purchase** within a month of launch.
- Boost became a reusable growth lever that product teams across Botim Money can plug into.
