## The problem

Chats were where Botim users spent their time, but the surface earned nothing. Businesses still reached the same users through SMS and other channels.

## How I approached it

- **Opened Botim chats to businesses.** Verified business accounts can send verification messages (OTPs) and promotional messages to users, inside the chat they already use.
- **Two products on one rail.** Verification messages for businesses that need to confirm a user, and promotional messages for advertisers, with a banner, short copy and one clear call to action.

## Outcome

- **Chat monetisation brought in approx 1.2M USD in revenue in its first 6 months after launch.**
