## The problem

Botim is used every day for calls and chats, but only a small share of those users ever touch its fintech services. Payments lived behind separate tabs, so the moments where money naturally comes up in a conversation (a birthday, a shared dinner, sending money home) were lost.

## How I approached it

- **Found the money moments in chat.** I mapped where users already talk about money and prioritised the ones with the highest intent: gifting, splitting a bill, collecting money for a group and sending money home.
- **Made the entry point part of the conversation.** A Dirham-icon tooltip, a pulsating payment call to action and contextual celebrations (birthday and congratulations animations that nudge a gift) put payments one tap away, inside the chat.
- **Brought money reminders into the chat list.** EasyCash repayments now show up as a Bill Reminder, and turn into a red Bill Overdue alert, right on the chat list, so users see what they owe where they spend their day.
- **Built one framework instead of many screens.** I designed a single transaction message-card framework that works for P2P, AANI, gifts and remittances, so every new transfer type ships as a configuration, not a rebuild.
- **Measured causally.** Every change ran as a controlled experiment with clean control groups, measured on incremental transacting users rather than raw clicks.

## Outcome

- **57K+ new users converted to fintech in a quarter** through the chat money moments.
- **3% uplift in EasyCash lending repayments** after Bill Reminder and Bill Overdue alerts moved into the chat list, with 7% of users opening those messages.
- The card framework is now the shared base for new transfer types across squads.
